Both mechs start at 10 HP. Each round the two models answer the same question independently and reveal at the same time. Fight lasts at most 14 rounds; the higher HP wins, and a tie goes to total damage dealt. Damage, HP, and arithmetic are all resolved in code, the models only make the choice.
